## About

I am a Software Engineering undergraduate at **Pujiang Institute, Nanjing Tech University**, with a primary focus on **computer vision, deep learning, and intelligent perception systems**.

My work explores how visual models can achieve a better balance between **accuracy, efficiency, robustness, and deployability**.

Current interests include object detection, semantic segmentation, multi-object tracking, medical image analysis, lightweight neural networks, and visual perception under complex real-world conditions.

Beyond model development, I also work on complete research and engineering pipelines, covering data processing, model training, quantitative evaluation, visualization, desktop applications, web systems, and deployment.

## Research Philosophy

> **Accuracy is only one dimension of a useful model.**

I am particularly interested in methods that remain computationally reasonable while preserving strong visual representation capability.

My projects generally emphasize three aspects:

**Performance** — improving detection, segmentation, tracking, or quantitative analysis capability.

**Efficiency** — reducing unnecessary parameters and computational overhead.

**Engineering** — transforming experimental models into reproducible and usable systems.
